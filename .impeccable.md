## Design Context

### Users
The author, on their own Mac, all day. Notes are quick captures (checklists, reminders) living in a floating deck at the screen edge; the main window is where they are browsed, searched and edited at length.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, handmade, inked. Kioku = 記憶 (memory). It should feel like a desk with paper notes on it, not like software.

### Aesthetic Direction
Japanese stationery: washi paper, sumi ink, a vermilion (朱) hanko seal as the only loud color, generous empty space (ma). Yomogi handwriting face everywhere, matching the floating deck. Notes keep their 8-color paper palette; the surrounding window is the desk (warm sumi in dark mode, warm washi in light mode). Anti-references: generic SwiftUI list with blue selection, cards with drop shadows everywhere, glassmorphism.

### Design Principles
1. The note is the only bright object; everything around it recedes.
2. Vermilion is reserved for the hanko (pinned). Nothing else uses it.
3. Lists are an index, not a card grid: swatch + title, no containers.
4. Motion decelerates and settles; no bounce.
5. Same objects, same look in the deck and the window.
